## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Three-ring hero
The Hub SHALL present a hero of three separate full-circle rings — **Fast**, **Food**, **Move** —
above the card stack. Each ring SHALL show a center value + a short domain label and a caption
below it, and SHALL use its domain accent for the arc and the matching track fill for the
background: Fast → `context.appColors.fast` on `fastTrack`, Food → `food` on `foodTrack`, Move →
`move` on `moveTrack`. Rings render as full circles (no bottom gap), theme-aware in both modes.

#### Scenario: Active values
- **WHEN** a fast is running, calories are logged, and steps are recorded
- **THEN** the Fast ring shows the remaining countdown, the Food ring shows calories against goal,
  and the Move ring shows steps against the step goal, each with its domain color

#### Scenario: Fast ring reflects fasting progress
- **WHEN** the fasting progress is P (0–1) toward the goal
- **THEN** the Fast ring's arc fill equals P and its center shows the countdown

### Requirement: Ring idle state
Each ring SHALL render an idle state — track only (no progress arc) plus a glyph or dimmed
placeholder in the center and the plain domain name as caption — when its source has not started
(no active fast / no calories logged / no steps).

#### Scenario: Nothing started
- **WHEN** no fast is active and no food or steps are logged today
- **THEN** all three rings show their idle (track-only) state without progress arcs

### Requirement: Food over-goal state
When calories exceed the goal, the Food ring SHALL switch to the danger color, fill fully, and
show the amount over (e.g. "+312 / OVER") rather than remaining calories.

#### Scenario: Over the calorie goal
- **WHEN** logged calories exceed the effective goal
- **THEN** the Food ring is danger-colored, full, and its center indicates the amount over goal

### Requirement: Hub header
The header SHALL show the greeting + date and a settings entry, plus a **streak pill** (flame icon
+ count). The streak reflects the app's day-streak source. Touch targets ≥44×44px.

#### Scenario: Header contents
- **WHEN** the Hub is shown
- **THEN** the greeting, current date, a streak pill, and a settings control are visible

### Requirement: Adaptive coaching line
The Hub SHALL show a single adaptive coaching sentence beneath the hero, generated by the AI coach,
with a mood tint (neutral / urgent / positive) reflecting the current state. Generation MUST run off
the build path: the Hub shows the cached last nudge (or a neutral fallback) immediately and updates
when generation completes; a generation failure keeps the fallback. No synchronous model call on
build.

#### Scenario: Coaching line renders without blocking
- **WHEN** the Hub builds
- **THEN** a coaching line (cached, fallback, or freshly generated) appears and the Hub remains
  responsive — it never blocks on model generation

#### Scenario: Generation failure falls back
- **WHEN** AI nudge generation fails
- **THEN** the Hub shows the neutral fallback line without error

### Requirement: Rich Finance card
The Finance card SHALL present a hero metric — the **forecasted ending cash balance**
(`forecastedNetBalance`: liquid minus unfunded set-asides, unpaid bills, and remaining budget), with
a net-worth variant at month-end — plus in/out cashflow bars, a savings-rate chip, and an
upcoming-bill countdown list, reusing existing treasury getters. The existing monthly summary and
embedded quick-log remain available.

#### Scenario: Finance overview
- **WHEN** the Finance card is shown mid-month
- **THEN** it shows a spend-headroom hero, inflow/outflow bars, a savings-rate chip, and upcoming
  bills with day counts

#### Scenario: Bill imminent emphasis
- **WHEN** a bill is due imminently
- **THEN** the Finance card surfaces it (ordering/emphasis) as it does today

### Requirement: Weight and Body pairing
Weight and Body measurement SHALL be presented together as a compact 2-up row, each showing its
latest value and trend delta.

#### Scenario: Compact tiles
- **WHEN** weight and body data exist
- **THEN** both appear side-by-side with value + trend delta

### Requirement: Card stack ordering and reorder retained
The redesigned Hub SHALL keep the existing card-ordering (active cards front-loaded) and
drag-to-reorder behavior for the cards below the hero. The three-ring hero remains fixed above the
reorderable list (it is not itself reorderable).

#### Scenario: Reorder still works
- **WHEN** the user drags a card in the stack
- **THEN** the order updates and persists, and the hero stays pinned above the stack

### Requirement: Preserve features absent from the reference
Features present today but not in Hub v3 SHALL NOT be removed: the **Activity** card (steps AND
distance/km), the **Nutrition macro breakdown** (protein/carbs/fat), and the **Stats/Character**
card. Steps additionally feed the Move ring, but the distance detail MUST remain reachable.

#### Scenario: Nothing dropped
- **WHEN** the redesigned Hub is shown
- **THEN** activity distance, nutrition macros, and the Stats/Character card are all still reachable

### Requirement: Graceful degradation
The Hub and its hero SHALL render without error when optional presenters (Nutrition, Activity,
Treasury) are absent — a ring with no source shows its idle state and cards with no data hide or
show empty states.

#### Scenario: Missing optional presenters
- **WHEN** the Nutrition and Activity presenters are null (e.g. a widget test)
- **THEN** the Hub renders, the Food/Move rings show idle, and no exception is thrown

### Requirement: Configurable hero rings and macro-split option
Which metric each of the three hero slots shows SHALL be configurable, and a **macro-split ring**
option SHALL be available: a segmented ring showing the proportion of protein / carbs / fat
(parts-of-a-whole, visually distinct from a progress ring), colored with the app's macro colors.
When the user does not use fasting (no fasting goal set, or no fast has ever been started), the
first slot SHALL default to the macro-split ring instead of the Fast ring; a setting SHALL allow
manual override of the slot configuration.

#### Scenario: Non-faster default
- **WHEN** the user has never started a fast (or fasting is disabled)
- **THEN** the first hero slot shows the macro-split ring instead of the Fast ring

#### Scenario: Macro proportions
- **WHEN** protein/carbs/fat are logged
- **THEN** the macro-split ring shows them as proportional segments summing to the whole

#### Scenario: Manual override
- **WHEN** the user changes the hero slot configuration in settings
- **THEN** the chosen metric renders in that slot on the Hub

### Requirement: Docked quick-log bar stays pinned
The quick-log bar SHALL stay pinned to the bottom of the Hub — it does not scroll away with the
card content — and continues routing typed input to nutrition vs. ledger as today.

#### Scenario: Stays put while scrolling
- **WHEN** the Hub card content is scrolled
- **THEN** the quick-log bar remains fixed at the bottom of the screen

### Requirement: Hub micro-actions
Cards SHALL offer inline micro-actions for the single most likely next action per card, reusing
existing presenter methods and without leaving the Hub. At minimum: Quests exposes a **Mark done**
control that completes the surfaced quest; the **Weight** tile reveals an **inline single-value
quick-entry on tap** (with Save and Cancel); the **Body** tile opens the **full body-measurement
entry** (multiple fields — waist, etc.), since a single inline field cannot capture it; an upcoming
bill exposes a **Pay** control.
Because paying mutates money, Pay MUST present a lightweight confirm before committing — never a
silent one-tap — showing the amount and a **selectable source account**, preselected to the bill's
usual / last-used funding account and changeable within the confirm. Micro-actions MUST be limited
to the primary action per card (no button farms) and MUST reflect the updated state immediately.

#### Scenario: Mark a quest done from the Hub
- **WHEN** the user taps "Mark done" on the surfaced quest
- **THEN** the quest is completed via the existing quest flow and the card updates without navigating away

#### Scenario: Inline weight entry
- **WHEN** the user taps the Weight tile
- **THEN** an inline single-value field appears with Save and Cancel; Save records it via the existing weight path and the tile updates, Cancel restores the tile unchanged

#### Scenario: Body opens the full entry
- **WHEN** the user taps the Body tile
- **THEN** the full multi-field body-measurement entry opens (not an inline field), because body tracks more than one measurement

#### Scenario: Paying a bill requires confirmation
- **WHEN** the user taps "Pay" on an upcoming bill
- **THEN** a confirm showing amount + source account is presented, and the payment is recorded only after the user confirms

#### Scenario: Change the source account
- **WHEN** the pay confirm is shown
- **THEN** a source account is preselected and the user can change it to another of their accounts before confirming; the payment posts against the chosen account

#### Scenario: No silent money mutation
- **WHEN** "Pay" is tapped but not confirmed
- **THEN** no payment is committed

### Requirement: Nudgr visual language and motion
The Hub SHALL use theme-aware colors only (no hardcoded `AppColors`/`AppColorsLight` in widgets),
render correctly in dark and light, keep touch targets ≥44×44px, and keep animations ≤400ms
(ring fills and transitions).

#### Scenario: Both themes
- **WHEN** the Hub is shown in dark and in light mode
- **THEN** all elements render correctly with theme-aware colors in both
